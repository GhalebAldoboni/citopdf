Cito PDF

The instant PDF reader for research. A free, open-source Chrome extension. Papers stream in and open at once, every citation pops up in place, and it stays light on any machine.

Download the latest release   Source on GitHub

What it does

  • Streaming, not downloading. A 200 MB PDF shows page 1 in 0.35 s; a 7 MB paper on a slow link in 0.55 s instead of 4.5 s. The rest downloads in the background.
  • Citation popups. Click [12] or (Vaswani et al., 2017) and the paper appears in place with its abstract, Cited by, Cite (APA, MLA, Chicago, BibTeX) and Save to Google Scholar library. A 39-page paper with 276 references is linked in 10 s.
  • Light on any machine. 61 fps pinch zoom, nothing rendered mid-fling, text layers only for visible pages, automatic tuning for 4 GB or dual-core laptops, memory released after five minutes idle.
  • Night mode and AMOLED black, vector printing, the PDF's real URL in the address bar, opens IEEE, ACM, Wiley, arXiv, ProQuest, EBSCO and local files. No analytics.

Install

  1. Download and unpack the zip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, pick the folder.
  3. Enable "Allow access to file URLs" for local PDFs; sign in to Google Scholar for Save.

Cito is Latin for "swiftly, at once". Say it aloud and you hear "cite".
