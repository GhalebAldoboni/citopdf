    Every paper you read lands here, with your place kept. Three ways to start:

    1. 1Open any PDF link in Chrome. Cito PDF takes over on its own.
    2. 2Open file above, or drop a PDF on this page.
    3. 3Paste a link, DOI or arXiv id into the box.

    PDFs you open in Chrome, from the web or from file://, appear here by themselves, with your place kept. Files opened with the button or by dropping are listed by name; Chrome may ask for permission again when you reopen one. This list never leaves your computer.

    What Cito PDF does

    • Instant streaming

      Page one appears while the file is still downloading, even at 200 MB.

    • Citation popups

      Click [12] and the cited paper opens in place, with Cite and Save.

    • Light on any machine

      Smooth zoom, idle-time rendering, and memory handed back when a tab rests.

    Tips

    • ShiftNCycle day, night and AMOLED black while reading.
    • Right-clickOn a page, copy it as an image or as text.
    • #gsr=0Add it to a PDF's address to use Chrome's own viewer.
    • CtrlClickOn a recent paper, open it in a new tab (Cmd on a Mac).
